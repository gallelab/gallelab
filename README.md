## Hi, I'm Galle

I'm a tour guide in Cali, Colombia, becoming a data analyst.

For years I've walked visitors through the city and the cloud forests of Valle del Cauca, and I kept hearing the same questions. Where do tourists actually go? What do they pay for? Why do some come back and others never do? Nobody was measuring any of it. So I'm learning to turn tourism into data, starting with the people I guide every week.

I also work at a dental radiology clinic, which taught me something every analyst needs: details matter, and records have to be right.

**Galle Lab** is where I build in public: data projects first, plus the 3D and educational experiments I can't stop making.

## What I'm building

| Project | What it is | Status |

| [**Cali Tourism Insights**](https://github.com/gallelab/cali-tourism-insights) | What do tourists really do in Cali? Activities, seasons and spending, analyzed with SQL and Python | In progress |

## Questions from the trail

The questions I bring from the tours into my analysis:

| The question | How I'd measure it |
|---|---|
| What do visitors spend their time and money on in Cali? | Bookings by activity, group size and price |
| When does demand for nature tourism peak? | Monthly bookings and seasonality |
| What is a rare bird worth to a local economy? | Visitor origin, trip length and spending |
